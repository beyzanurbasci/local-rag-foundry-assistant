Grand Horizon Hotel is a luxury 5-star resort located in Antalya, featuring 250 sea-view rooms, three outdoor swimming pools, a private beach, and a fully equipped wellness spa open daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Check-in time at Grand Horizon Hotel begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out time is strictly by 11:00 AM. Express check-in and check-out services are available at the front desk, which operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Late check-out until 3:00 PM can be requested subject to availability and carries a half-day room charge.

The resort offers multiple dining options, including the main international buffet restaurant open for breakfast (7:00 AM - 10:30 AM) and dinner (7:00 PM - 10:00 PM), alongside two à la carte restaurants specializing in Mediterranean seafood and Italian cuisine, requiring advance table reservations through the concierge.

Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is provided throughout the hotel property, including all guest rooms, pool areas, and the lobby lounge. Connection details and guest login instructions are printed on the room key card holder.

The hotel provides a complimentary airport shuttle bus service for all guests who book suites directly through the official website. Standard room guests can request airport transfer services for an additional fee by contacting the concierge desk at least 24 hours in advance.

Grand Horizon Hotel maintains a strict pet-friendly policy only for designated garden-level villas, requiring an advance reservation and a 30-euro nightly cleaning fee. Pets are not permitted in the main indoor hotel buildings, restaurants, or near the main swimming pool areas.

Room types include Deluxe Sea View Rooms, Family Suites, and Executive Garden Villas. All rooms are equipped with individually controlled air conditioning, electronic safes, minibars refilled daily, tea and coffee setup, and 43-inch smart LED televisions.

The wellness spa center offers a traditional Turkish hammam, sauna, steam rooms, and a wide range of therapeutic massages and facial treatments. Access to the indoor pool, sauna, and fitness center is completely free for all registered hotel guests, while spa massages require a paid reservation.

For families traveling with children, the "Horizon Kids Club" operates daily from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM for children aged 4 to 12, offering supervised indoor games, art workshops, and mini-disco activities free of charge. Professional babysitting services can also be booked through reception with a 12-hour prior notice for an hourly rate.

Parking facilities include a secure subterranean multi-story garage and an outdoor parking lot. Valet parking is available 24/7 at the main entrance. Self-parking and valet services are completely free of charge for all hotel guests during their stay.

The cancellation and refund policy allows free cancellation up to 48 hours before the scheduled check-in date for standard reservations. Cancellations made within 48 hours of arrival or no-shows will incur a charge equal to the first night's accommodation fee. Prepaid non-refundable promotional rates are strictly non-cancellable and non-refundable.

Room service is available 24 hours a day, offering a diverse menu ranging from late-night snacks and gourmet burgers to full course meals and children's selections. A standard 15% service fee is automatically added to all in-room dining bills.
